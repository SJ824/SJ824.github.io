    Full-Stack E2E Development
    Developed an End-to-End autonomous driving model from scratch, covering the entire pipeline from architecture design to implementation.
    Robustness
    Applied Imitation Learning and DAgger (Dataset Aggregation) to handle covariate shift and out-of-distribution scenarios.
